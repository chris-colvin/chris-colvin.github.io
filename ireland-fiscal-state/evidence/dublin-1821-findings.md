# HC 1821 (420): Dublin Taxes

Read all 12 PDF pages of user-supplied 1821-007419_complete-DublinTaxes1814-1820.pdf. Ordered printed 17 April 1821. Extracted page text in this directory. Figures below are reported pounds, currency not yet resolved.

- Printed p.2, paving, cleansing and lighting, year ending 5 January 1821: payable £40,701 15s 9d; received £32,204 4s 4d. Visually verified. This return distinguishes paving commissioners from grand-jury cess administration.
- Printed p.3, water accounts stop in 1819: pipe-water receipts £10,229 17s; metal-main receipts £9,818 11s. Note says 1820 collection still underway. Do not combine these as 1820 observations.
- Printed p.4, watch: assessed £12,276 1s 10d for 1820; received to 1 January 1821 £4,599 7s 9d. Collection continued to March 1822. This is not final yield.
- Printed p.5, wide streets, 1820: city assessed £7,548 9s 4d; received £4,944 7s 6d. County-area receipts £856 2s 6d. Visually verified. Explicitly assessed through grand juries; check overlap before adding to national grand-jury totals.
- Printed p.6, Foundling Hospital, year ending 5 January 1821: payable £8,731 2s 11d; received £7,538 5s 3d.
- Printed p.8, Anna Liffey cess: collected through grand-jury collectors; requires overlap check too.

Interpretation: detailed return is useful for identifying components and collection difficulties, not a clean single-year national extrapolation base. Earlier HC 1822 (394) aggregate £120,046 assessed and £94,837 collected must be reconciled, not mechanically recreated by adding last rows here. Revise any previous implication that all fixed taxes are necessarily outside grand-jury returns.

National comparison: Hansard 9 December 1830, Grand Juries (Ireland), Newport reports £755,000 levied in 1821 from parliamentary returns. Dublin city grand-jury assessment £24,533 in 1820 is roughly 3.2% of this, but adjacent years, currency and levy definitions require confirmation. It is not a share of all local taxation. https://hansard.parliament.uk/Commons/1830-12-09/debates/f6d81e0d-483d-46d9-ac80-7ba291015a3d/GrandJuries(Ireland)
