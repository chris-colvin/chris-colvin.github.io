After the Union: Ireland Inside the World’s First Industrial Economy, 1801–1901

Christopher L. Colvin and Eoin McLaughlin

This book examines Ireland’s economic development within the nineteenth-century United Kingdom. We argue that the timing of the Union mattered as much as its terms: integration into an industrialising economy shaped opportunities around inequalities of property and political power, regional specialisation and established industrial centres.

Famine, emigration and institutional reform transformed Irish society. Living standards converged towards British levels, but industry remained geographically concentrated and sectorally narrow. Combining economic history with cliometric analysis, we explain how adaptation brought greater prosperity without broad industrial transformation, and how political organisation reshaped opportunities.

Publication is scheduled for 2027 with Cambridge University Press. Currently on seminar tour.
