After the Union: Ireland Inside the World’s First Industrial Economy, 1801–1901

Christopher L. Colvin and Eoin McLaughlin

This book examines Ireland’s economic development within the United Kingdom during the nineteenth century. We argue that the timing of Union mattered as much as its terms. Ireland joined an already industrialising economy, and its development depended on how shared institutions worked in practice: how they were administered and financed, whom they served, and how they operated under different local conditions.

The Great Famine, emigration and institutional reform reshaped Irish society. Living standards improved, but economic change varied across regions and sectors. Drawing on historical scholarship and quantitative evidence, we examine how these developments were connected and what they reveal about Ireland’s place within the United Kingdom.

Publication is scheduled for 2027 with Cambridge University Press. Currently on seminar tour.
